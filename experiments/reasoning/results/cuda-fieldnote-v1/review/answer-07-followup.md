**1. Which system meets the daily council requirement?**
**Cedar** delivers 1,200 liters per day, exceeding the 1,000-liter requirement. Birch only delivers 900 liters per day after the audit corrected the brochure's inflated claim of 1,400 liters [trial.md](file:///data/trial.md).

Cedar's operating cost is **18 credits per day**, plus a mandatory 400-credit inlet upgrade [cost.md](file:///data/cost.md).

**3. Which component does the orange route bypass?**
The orange maintenance bypass route skips the **UV chamber**, flowing directly from Intake to Storage [Harbor Water Pilot](attachment://9a503717ac5a/page/2).
