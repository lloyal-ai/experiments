## Harbor Water Pilot Selection Brief

### Inquiry 1: Capacity and Energy Comparison (corpus_research)

**Cedar vs. Birch Performance:**
- **Cedar**: 1200 liters/day capacity, 6 kWh/day energy use (trial.md)
- **Birch**: 900 liters/day capacity, 3 kWh/day energy use (trial.md)

**Corrected brochure claim:** The brochure claimed Birch delivered 1400 liters/day, but the independent audit corrected this to 900 liters/day, noting the brochure used a "short clean-water test that is not representative" (audit.md).

**Council requirement:** At least 1000 liters/day (audit.md)

**Conclusion:** Only **Cedar meets the capacity requirement** at 1200 liters/day. Birch alone fails at 900 liters/day.

---

### Inquiry 2: Operating Cost and Feasibility (corpus_research)

**Operating costs (cost.md):**
- **Cedar**: 18 credits/day + 400-credit inlet upgrade required
- **Birch**: 12 credits/day + no inlet upgrade required

**Installation constraints:** Cedar requires a 400-credit inlet upgrade; Birch needs none. A grant covers installation but not recurring operating costs.

**Reconciliation:** While Birch has lower operating costs (12 vs 18 credits/day), it fails the capacity requirement. Cedar's additional 400-credit installation cost is offset by the grant, but the daily 18-credit operating cost remains.

**Feasible option:** **Cedar** is the only system meeting the 1000-liter/day requirement at 18 credits/day operating cost.

---

### Inquiry 3: Treatment Routing Figure Analysis (document_research)

**Visual explanation of Figure 1 (page 2):**
- **Blue solid line** = normal treatment route: Intake → UV chamber → Storage
- **Orange dashed line** = maintenance bypass: Intake → Storage (skipping UV chamber)

**Component bypassed:** The orange maintenance route bypasses the **UV chamber**. During maintenance of the UV chamber, water flows directly from Intake to Storage without UV treatment.

---

### Inquiry 4: Maintenance and Unknowns (corpus_research)

**Maintenance intervals (maintenance.md):**
- **Cedar**: Filter inspection every 7 days
- **Birch**: Filter inspection every 14 days

**Limitations and unknowns:**
- Pilot measured **no winter performance** data
- Neither system has established annual reliability from this 30-day pilot
- Do not treat a 30-day result as an annual guarantee

**What is unknown:** Winter reliability for both systems remains unmeasured.

---

## Final Recommendation

**Select Cedar system** because it is the only one meeting the 1000-liter/day council requirement at 18 credits/day operating cost. The orange maintenance route bypasses the UV chamber during servicing. Winter reliability remains unknown for both systems.
