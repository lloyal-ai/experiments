**Which system meets the daily council requirement?**
**Cedar** meets the requirement. The trial results show Cedar delivered **1200 liters per day**, while Birch delivered only 900 liters per day. The council requires at least 1000 liters per day, so Cedar alone satisfies this requirement, whereas Birch alone does not.

**What is its daily operating cost?**
Cedar's daily operating cost is **18 credits per day** (excluding installation).

**Which component does the orange route bypass?**
The orange route bypasses the **UV chamber**. According to Figure 1: Treatment routing on page 2, the orange dashed line shows the maintenance bypass route that goes from Intake directly to Storage, skipping the UV chamber entirely.
