## Harbor Water Pilot Selection Brief

### 1. Which System Meets the Council Requirement?

**Cedar** is the only system meeting the council's daily requirement.

- **Council requirement**: At least 1000 liters per day [audit.md](file:///data/corpus/audit.md)
- **Cedar capacity**: 1200 liters per day [trial.md](file:///data/corpus/trial.md)
- **Birch capacity**: 900 liters per day [trial.md](file:///data/corpus/trial.md)

The independent 30-day audit corrected an early brochure claim that Birch delivered 1400 liters per day down to 900 liters per day, noting the brochure used a short clean-water test that is not representative. Birch's 900 liters/day falls short by 100 liters of the minimum requirement.

### 2. Daily Operating Cost

**Cedar's daily operating cost**: 18 credits per day

- Cedar operating cost: 18 credits/day [cost.md](attachment://cost.md)
- Birch operating cost: 12 credits/day [cost.md](attachment://cost.md)

Cedar requires a 400-credit inlet upgrade (one-time installation cost), while Birch requires no upgrade.

### 3. Component Bypassed by the Orange Route

**The orange route bypasses the UV chamber**.

From the Harbor Water Pilot routing diagram on page 2:
- **Blue route** (normal treatment): Intake → UV chamber → Storage
- **Orange route** (maintenance bypass): Intake → skips UV chamber → Storage

The orange dashed line shows water flowing from Intake directly to Storage, completely bypassing the UV chamber component.

---

## Conclusion

Cedar must be selected because it is the only system meeting the council's 1000-liter daily capacity requirement. Birch's 900 liters/day falls short by 100 liters, a gap that cannot be bridged by operational adjustments. The higher operating cost (18 credits/day vs. 12 credits/day) and inlet upgrade requirement (400 credits) are quantifiable tradeoffs, but Cedar's capacity compliance is non-negotiable.

### Limitations

The pilot evidence has critical gaps:
- **Winter reliability**: No winter performance was measured [maintenance.md](file:///content/maintenance.md)
- **Annual forecasts**: Trial figures refer to identical operating conditions, not annual projections [trial.md](file:///data/corpus/trial.md)
- **Unknown costs**: Actual maintenance labor/replacement costs and energy pricing remain unknown [cost.md](attachment://cost.md)
