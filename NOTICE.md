# Source and notices

This repository includes Lloyal benchmark code, a pinned Fieldnote application snapshot, adapted HDK content helpers and versioned ability/media archives. The Lloyal source license is retained in [LICENSE](LICENSE); component notices are preserved under `experiments/memory/reference/licenses/` and inside the vendored archives. Third-party dependencies retain their respective licenses.

Native engines are fetched by exact revision during setup rather than vendored into Git. Their original licenses remain in those checkouts. Instrumentation is distributed as reviewable patches alongside its source identities.
